Support

Effective: November 15, 2026

Restoring purchases

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account, then open Settings → Restore purchases. An internet connection is needed.

Moving to a new phone

Restore your old device's backup to the new one. The app has no accounts or cloud sync of its own.

The water is cloudy

A new tank takes three to four weeks to settle. Add fish one group at a time meanwhile, and change the water when it clouds. Feeding too often fouls the water faster.

Photos are not in Photos

On iPhone allow "Add Photos" in Settings → Mulsari and save again. On Android check the Pictures/Mulsari folder in your gallery.

No notifications

Check Care reminders and Moments in the app's settings, and your phone's notification settings. Reminders come in the daytime, at most once a day.

Refunds

iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: your order history in Google Play.

Contact

Email studiojinwoo@gmail.com with the app version, your phone model and OS version, and what happened. We never need passwords or card details.
