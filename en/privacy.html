Privacy

Effective: November 15, 2026

In short

Mulsari has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It sends anonymous technical information when the app crashes, and anonymous usage counts only if you agree.

Stored on your device

Your tanks and fish, their names, the sightings log, cards, photos, settings and purchase entitlements stay on your device. The developer cannot see them. Deleting the app removes its local records; photos you saved to Photos or your gallery remain.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error it sends technical information (the error, app version, device model and OS, and a random identifier created per install) to Unity Technologies' diagnostics service. It never sends names, photos, tank records, location or contacts. This is used only to fix problems.

Usage statistics (optional)

After about three days the app asks once. Only if you choose "Happy to help" does it send which features are used and how often (for example fish brought home, leave-on time) with an anonymous identifier to Unity Technologies' analytics service. Turn it off anytime in Settings → Anonymous usage stats; turning it off also asks for the data already sent to be deleted. It is never used for advertising or tracking across apps.

Service provider

Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: diagnostics and analytics), under Unity's privacy policy. Data is encrypted in transit. Crash information is kept only as long as needed to fix problems (at most one year).

Purchases

Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes purchases, transaction checks and restores. The developer never receives card numbers or other payment details.

Photos

On iPhone the app asks for add-only access to Photos only when you choose to save a photo; it never reads your existing photos. On Android 10 and later it saves to a Mulsari folder in your gallery without any permission.

Notifications

Notifications are off at first; the permission is requested only when you turn them on in Settings. They are scheduled on your device: care reminders at most once a day, moments at most twice a week. No server push, no promotional notifications.

Children

The app collects no names, contacts or location from anyone, including children. Purchases follow Apple and Google purchase approval and parental settings.

Email support

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and resolve your request, and delete them one year after the final reply.

Your rights

You can turn off usage statistics and request deletion in the app's settings. You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your support correspondence at the contact address. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

This website

This website has no cookies, tracking code or external scripts. GitHub Pages may process technical access logs, such as IP addresses, to operate its hosting service.
