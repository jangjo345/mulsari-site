Mulsari

Mulsari is a small aquarium that lives in your phone. Cycle the water, feed your fish, and wait for the day your guppies have fry. Stand your phone on its charger and your room has a little tank.

  • Six freshwater fish, five reef fish, corals and an anemone
  • A new tank takes weeks to settle, just like a real one
  • At night the lights go down and the fish sleep
  • No ads, no subscription
